Privacy notice

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This notice describes information BookedBack collects through the pilot request form and how it is used. BookedBack is an early-stage, founder-run service.

Information collected

If you request a conversation, we collect the company name, your name, work email, approximate weekly new-customer call volume if provided, the operational issue you describe, and the date of your request. The form is not intended for homeowner or other consumer information.

How information is used

We use these business details to review fit for the 30-day pilot and contact you about your request. We do not use the form to collect call recordings, homeowner names, telephone numbers, addresses, account credentials, or payment-card information. Do not submit those details.

Storage and access

The public site is delivered by Vercel. Pilot requests are forwarded to BookedBack’s existing hosted database and appear in the operator’s private lead workspace. The workspace is intended for the BookedBack operator. We do not sell request information. The website does not use advertising pixels or analytics code in this application. Hosting providers may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the service under their own terms.

Retention and requests

Request details are kept while needed to respond and manage the pilot relationship. A hashed value used to limit repeated form submissions is automatically removed after a few days. This early service does not yet have automatic retention or self-service deletion for inquiry records. To request access, correction, or deletion, reply to the BookedBack email about your inquiry and identify the company and request date. The operator will handle the request manually.

Security and changes

BookedBack limits access to the owner workspace and uses hosted service infrastructure. No online system can be represented as risk-free. This notice may change as the service develops; the date above will be updated when it does.

This short notice explains the current form and internal workflow; it is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review or a signed pilot agreement.
